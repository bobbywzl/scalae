# Li Lu — Primary-Source Corpus

> Scalae corpus library · file 08 of 09 · compiled 2026-08-12 · every link verified at compile time unless tagged [unverified-fetch] / [paywall] · [zh] marks Chinese-language originals · see `00-INDEX.md` for tier semantics and ingestion order.

Li Lu (李录, b. 1966) — Tiananmen student leader turned founder of Himalaya Capital (Seattle), the only outside manager Charlie Munger trusted with his family's money and Munger's self-described intellectual heir in China; he is the canonical living bridge between Graham–Buffett–Munger value investing and China's modernization story.

## Voice profile — how Li Lu thinks
- **Value investing as fiduciary truth-seeking**: asset management is a profession with "priest-like" ethical duty; the investor's first obligation is intellectual honesty — knowing the boundary of what you truly know (2015 Peking University lecture; 2019 "The Practice of Value Investing").
- **Four pillars, endlessly restated in his own vocabulary**: stocks are fractional ownership of real businesses; Mr. Market is a servant to be used, never a master; margin of safety; and the circle of competence — "true knowledge" proven by action (知行合一), not opinion (2015, 2019, 2024 lectures).
- **Civilization 1.0/2.0/3.0 framework**: hunter-gatherer → agrarian → modern science-technology civilization; sustained compound economic growth is a uniquely "Civilization 3.0" phenomenon created by free markets plus modern science — this is the macro engine that makes long-term equity ownership rational (A Discussion of Modernization, 2014; 2015 lecture).
- **China's modernization arc**: China excelled at Agrarian-era institutions (keju meritocracy), lagged into 3.0, and can escape the middle-income trap only by staying inside the market order and building a modern capital market — hence value investing has a real future in China (2014, Dec 2018, Dec 2024).
- **"Wealth is a share of purchasing power in the economy"** — so own dynamic companies inside vibrant economies; "the macro is what we must accept; the micro is where we can and should make a difference" (Global Value Investing in Our Era, 2024).
- **Research as investigative obsession, temperament as contrarian patience**: walk the stores, count the receipts (Timberland case, 2006 Columbia lecture); sit in cash for years; "occasionally" look at prices; opportunities are seized in crisis (2006, 2010 Columbia lectures; Graham & Doddsville 2013).
- **Munger as moral template**: make money you could "comfortably take the other side of"; inversion; study failure; rationality as lifelong discipline — Munger as "a businessman with a Literati's soul" (商才士魂), modern-day Confucianism (2010 PCA foreword; 2023 eulogy).

## Ingestion notes
- **Highest voice-per-token**: the lecture trilogy — 2015 "Prospect" (~30k words), 2019 "Practice" + Q&A (~21k words), 2024 "Global Value Investing in Our Era" (~31 pp incl. Q&A) — plus the 2010 PCA foreword and 2013 Graham & Doddsville interview. Together they contain nearly every signature idea; the 2014 modernization treatise (~60k words) adds the historical framework at lower investing-density.
- **Rough corpus size**: ~150–200k English words plus Chinese originals of near-equal length; small enough to ingest whole.
- **Translation authority**: himcap.com translations are official and authoritative for the 2015 lecture, 2014/2018 modernization essays, PCA foreword, 2023 Munger eulogy, and 2024 speech. The 2019 lecture and its Q&A exist **only** in the unofficial LONGRIVER (Graham Rhodes) translation — label as unofficial but book-aligned.
- **Dedup risk is high**: the 2015 lecture circulates as official "The Prospect…" (himcap), LONGRIVER's "The Prospects…", and Off-Piste Investing's version — same speech, three renderings; the Dec 2024 speech was virally reposted in Jan 2025 (Sina, QQ, GuruFocus, Acquirer's Multiple excerpts) — treat all as duplicates of the official PDF; the 2021 Greenwald conversation has two independent transcripts (Latticework, roiss).
- **Transcript quality caveats**: 2006 and 2010 Columbia lectures are fan transcriptions from poor audio — the 2010 GuruFocus text is explicitly an "approximation," and the 2006 roiss text is edited for readability (cross-checked against Li Lu's own Chinese book text); prefer official PDFs verbatim, treat fan transcripts as near-verbatim.
- **Fetch caveat**: copies of the 2015-lecture and 2024-speech zh URLs circulating elsewhere often carry a mangled percent-encoding (仳 %E4%BB%B3 in place of 价 %E4%BB%B7) and 403 — the corrected URLs above fetch clean (verified 2026-08-12). The 2018 zh essay PDF may still 403 datacenter IPs; fall back to the himcap zh publications page or the verified Sina full-text mirror for the 2024 speech.

# Shared Archives — Multi-Investor Primary Sources

> Scalae corpus library · file 09 of 09 · compiled 2026-08-12 · every link verified at compile time unless tagged [unverified-fetch] / [paywall] · see `00-INDEX.md` for tier semantics and ingestion order.

The cross-cutting publications, media archives, and libraries that carry several canon investors' own words (interviews, lectures, letters, testimony) in one place — with per-subject coverage and harvest routes. Verified August 2026. (Buffett/Munger appear in most of these too but are cataloged elsewhere.)

## Tier 1 — Highest-yield archives
- **[Graham & Doddsville](https://business.columbia.edu/heilbrunn/resources/graham-and-doddsville-newsletter)** — 2006–present · free PDFs, 2–3 issues/yr, 3–5 long-form interviews each · free — Columbia Business School's student investment newsletter (Heilbrunn Center). Confirmed for our nine, verified inside the issue PDFs: **Joel Greenblatt** — Issue 16, Fall 2012, "Thought Process and Clarity are Key" ([PDF](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/imce-uploads/Graham%20%26%20Doddsville%20-%20Issue%2016%20-%20Fall%202012_vFINAL2.pdf)); **Mohnish Pabrai** — Fall 2008 ([standalone PDF](http://www.grahamanddoddsville.net/wordpress/Files/BLArticles/An%20Interview%20with%20Mohnish%20Pabrai%20-%20Graham%20and%20Doddsville%20-%20Fall%202008.pdf)) and again Issue 37, Fall 2019 ([PDF](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/imce-uploads/Graham%26Doddsville_Issue37.pdf)); **Li Lu** — Issue 18, Spring 2013 ([Himalaya-hosted copy](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5ef3c7300432b40ed865991a/642e161699ad88498e9c681a_2013-03-28%20Graham%20&%20Doddsville%20Article_LL.pdf)). No Klarman interview exists (site search shows only passing mentions); Graham/Dodd/Schloss/Templeton/Lynch were never interviewees. Harvest: the official index page 403s scripted crawlers [unverified-fetch for the index only] but the PDFs on business.columbia.edu download freely; the unofficial **[mirror archive](https://www.grahamanddoddsville.net/?page_id=689)** lists every issue Winter 2006/07–Spring 2018 with direct PDF links.
- **[Heilbrunn Center Archives](https://business.columbia.edu/heilbrunn/resources/archives)** — 1946–present materials · free PDFs + finding aids + video · free — the single best Graham primary-text trove online: all **10 of Benjamin Graham's "Current Problems in Security Analysis" lectures (Sept 1946–Feb 1947)** as PDFs (verified, predictable URL pattern: [Lecture 1](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/imce-uploads/Graham_Sept1946Feb1947_CurrentProblemsinSecurityAnalysis_Lecture1.pdf)), **Graham-Newman partnership letters/returns 1946–1958**, plus pointers to the physical **[Benjamin Graham Papers](https://findingaids.library.columbia.edu/ead/nnc-rb/ldpd_17325026)** and **[Schloss Family Business Papers](https://findingaids.library.columbia.edu/ead/nnc-rb/ldpd_12851449)** at Columbia RBML (finding-aids site blocks scripted fetches; links taken from the Heilbrunn page). Also a [presentations video archive](https://business.columbia.edu/heilbrunn/resources/archives/presentations) (CSIMA conference, recent years) and the [Roger Murray lecture series](https://business.columbia.edu/heilbrunn/resources/archives/roger-murray-lecture-series). Covers: Graham (deep), Schloss (papers), Dodd (name/context only). Harvest: bulk-download the lecture and Graham-Newman PDFs by URL pattern.
- **[Value Investing with Legends](https://business.columbia.edu/heilbrunn/resources/value-investing-legends-podcast)** — 2019–present · free podcast, 77+ episodes · free — Columbia/Heilbrunn's faculty-hosted podcast (Tano Santos, Michael Mauboussin). Confirmed episodes: **Joel Greenblatt**, "Investing Off the Beaten Path" (Nov 15, 2019); **Mohnish Pabrai**, "The Value of Continuous Learning" (Jan 15, 2021); **Seth Klarman**, "Contrarian Investing, Discipline, and Building Baupost" (Aug 1, 2025 — a rare long Klarman interview). Harvest: full back-catalog on [Libsyn](https://valueinvestingwithlegends.libsyn.com/) and [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/value-investing-with-legends/id1459993408); no official transcripts — auto-transcribe the MP3s.
- **[Talks at Google (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZfPJCAVQg0)** — 2015–2017 for our subjects · free video + captions — Google's speaker series. Confirmed: **Joel Greenblatt**, "The Little Book that Beats the Market" (2017, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZfPJCAVQg0)); **Mohnish Pabrai**, "Dhandho. Heads I Win; Tails I Don't Lose Much" (2017, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_nWM4vjgqE)); **Pabrai & Guy Spier** in conversation with Saurabh Madaan (2015, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBWWseoqBsc)). MOI Global hosts free edited transcripts of the 2017 talks (verified: [Pabrai](https://moiglobal.com/mohnish-pabrai-2017/), [Greenblatt](https://moiglobal.com/joel-greenblatt-2017/)). Harvest: YouTube captions + MOI transcripts.
- **[WealthTrack with Consuelo Mack](https://wealthtrack.com)** — 2005–present · weekly TV interview show; video free, transcripts per-episode · mostly free — the richest recurring TV source for **Joel Greenblatt**, with at least four episodes: ["2 Secrets to Beating the Market"](https://wealthtrack.com/great-value-investor-joel-greenblatt-reveals-the-2-secrets-to-beating-the-market/) (Ep. #1542, Apr 5, 2019 — verified; transcript downloadable from the page), ["Hybrid Investing"](https://wealthtrack.com/greenblatt-hybrid-investing/), ["Strategy Change"](https://wealthtrack.com/greenblatt-strategy-change/), and ["A Legendary Value Investor Mixes Passive with Active"](https://wealthtrack.com/a-legendary-value-investor-mixes-passive-with-active-to-overcome-destructive-investor-behavior/). No Templeton/Klarman/Pabrai episodes found on the site. Harvest: browse episode pages (YouTube embeds + SoundCloud audio); newest content gates early access behind WealthTrack Premium [paywall for early access/some transcripts].
- **[csinvesting.org](http://csinvesting.org)** — ~2011–2019 blog · thousands of hosted PDFs/case studies · free [caution] — John Chew's teaching archive, historically the home of **Joel Greenblatt's Columbia "special situations" class notes**, **Walter Schloss** documents (memos, OID excerpts, "16 golden rules"), and **Benjamin Graham** lectures/articles, organized into free courses. Current state is degraded: the homepage now serves spam content (likely domain compromise) while some deep pages (e.g., [free courses category](http://csinvesting.org/category/free-courses/)) still load and others 403. Harvest via **[Wayback Machine captures](https://web.archive.org/web/2023/http://csinvesting.org/)** rather than the live site.

## Tier 2 — Important archives
- **[SEC EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/)** — 2001–present · structured filings · free — verified UI and JSON API (efts.sec.gov). Primary *documents* (not interviews) for four subjects' firms: **Baupost Group** (Klarman) 13F-HRs, **Gotham Asset Management** (Greenblatt), **Himalaya Capital Management** (Li Lu; 74 full-text hits), **Dalal Street / Pabrai Investment Funds** (Pabrai), plus occasional 13D/G letters. Note: Baupost's famous partnership letters are NOT on EDGAR (but The Baupost Fund's 1996–2001 N-30D letters are — see the Klarman file). Harvest: query the API by company/CIK, form type 13F-HR.
- **[Masters in Business (Bloomberg/Ritholtz)](https://ritholtz.com/2020/10/transcript-joel-greenblatt-2/)** — 2014–present · podcast + free full transcripts — **Joel Greenblatt** at least twice: ["The Thrill of Investing"](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/audio/2018-04-20/joel-greenblatt-discusses-the-thrill-of-investing) (Apr 2018) and "Relative Value Investing" (Oct 2020, [full free transcript verified](https://ritholtz.com/2020/10/transcript-joel-greenblatt-2/)); **Seth Klarman** in June 2026 ([transcript](https://ritholtz.com/2026/06/transcript-seth-klarman/)). Harvest: Ritholtz posts transcripts on ritholtz.com — prefer those over audio scraping.
- **[Ben Graham Centre for Value Investing video library (Ivey, Western University)](https://www.ivey.uwo.ca/bengrahaminvesting/resources/video-library/)** — 2005–2026 · free YouTube-embedded videos — class guest talks + annual conference keynotes. Confirmed **Mohnish Pabrai** three times: 2012 Ivey class talk, 2021 conference panel, 2025 European conference fireside chat; also **Walter Schloss**'s 2008 class talk (see the Schloss file). Harvest: page-by-page browse; videos free on YouTube.
- **[MOI Global / The Manual of Ideas](https://moiglobal.com)** — 2008–present · membership community (currently waitlisted) with substantial free content — free "Selected Transcripts" section includes the verified **Pabrai** and **Greenblatt** 2017 Talks-at-Google transcripts (above) and the **Peter Lynch 1994** Press Club transcript; members get conference replays (Best Ideas, Zurich Project) and back issues of The Manual of Ideas journal, which repeatedly featured **Pabrai**. [paywall for member archive; meaningful free layer]
- **[Value Investor Insight](https://valueinvestorinsight.com/BrowseByYear_Issues.aspx)** — 2004–present · ~monthly interview-driven newsletter (founded by Whitney Tilson & John Heins) · [paywall] — the deepest paid archive of manager interviews in the style of our canon; back issues purchasable individually via "BUY ISSUE" on the [browse-by-year archive](https://valueinvestorinsight.com/BrowseByYear_Issues.aspx), with [free samples after registration](https://valueinvestorinsight.com/SampleView.aspx) (recent issues only). Greenblatt endorses the publication; whether it holds interviews of the nine (vs. the broader value community) could not be confirmed from outside the paywall — check the subscriber search before buying. Harvest: subscribe, or buy targeted back issues; do not rip full issues.
- **[Outstanding Investor Digest](https://www.oid.com)** — 1986–2012 · legendary interview digest · [dormant] [paywall] — publication stopped with the May 25, 2012 edition (Vol. XXV, Nos. 1 & 2), which carried a **Walter Schloss** tribute; OID's back catalog contains the canonical long **Walter & Edwin Schloss** interviews plus **Klarman** and **Templeton** Q&As (and decades of Buffett/Munger meeting coverage, cataloged elsewhere). No online archive: back issues only "by inquiry" via the site — email the publisher for subscriber-only back-issue offers; stray single-interview PDFs online are unlicensed rips, avoid.
- **[Charlie Rose archive](https://charlierose.com/guests/5687)** — 1991–2017 show archive · free streaming video — verified guest page for **Peter Lynch** with appearances Mar 4, 1993 ("Beating the Street"), Oct 28, 1997, and Dec 4, 2013; **John Templeton** May 14, 1997 (with official transcript); **Seth Klarman** Nov 2011 (circulates on YouTube). Harvest: stream per-episode pages; transcripts on newer pages only — otherwise transcribe audio.

## Tier 3 — Supplementary
- **[FRASER (St. Louis Fed digital library)](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org)** — 1830s–present · full-text-searchable scans · free — hosts the *Commercial and Financial Chronicle* full text, which printed contemporaneous coverage of **Benjamin Graham's March 1955 Senate "Stock Market Study" testimony** (e.g., [March 24, 1955 issue](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/commercial-financial-chronicle-1339/march-24-1955-556027/fulltext)) and decades of Graham-era speeches/articles; the complete 1955 hearing volume itself is locatable via FRASER's on-site search [unverified-fetch — /search endpoint rejected scripted queries] alongside related items like [Fed Chair Martin's statement at the same hearings](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/statements-speeches-william-mcchesney-martin-jr-448/statement-hearings-study-stock-market-senate-committee-banking-currency-7794). Graham-only among our nine. (The full GPO hearing volume is a free Google Books ebook — see the Graham & Dodd file.)
- **[Novel Investor quote library](https://novelinvestor.com/quotes/)** — ongoing · free, source-cited compilations — dedicated, citation-linked quote pages for **Graham, Templeton, Schloss, Lynch, Klarman, Greenblatt, Pabrai** (no Li Lu, no Dodd), plus a post series excerpting **Peter Lynch's Worth magazine columns (1992–1999)** and [Templeton's 22 maxims](https://novelinvestor.com/maxims-john-templeton/). Use as a pointer/dedup index to originals, not as primary text itself.
- **[Worth magazine — Peter Lynch author page](https://worth.com/plynch/)** — 1992–1999 columns · official but thin — Worth's own site republishes only one Lynch column ("Fear of Crashing," from the 1992 launch issue, reposted 2017). The full column run survives via library databases and Wayback captures of worth.com (see the Lynch file) [avoid the circulating rip PDFs].
- **[American Archive of Public Broadcasting — Wall $treet Week catalog search](https://americanarchive.org/catalog?q=%22wall+street+week%22)** — 1970s–2000s · free streaming of digitized public-TV episodes · [unverified-fetch — catalog blocks crawlers via robots.txt] — reported home of Maryland Public Television's *Wall $treet Week with Louis Rukeyser* collection, whose guest roster over 30+ years included **John Templeton** (recurring) and **Peter Lynch**; the best institutional hope for Templeton on-camera primary material. Browse manually on-site to confirm specific episodes before building a harvest list.
- **[Financial Analysts Journal (CFA Institute, via Taylor & Francis)](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ufaj20)** — 1945–present · academic archive · [paywall] [unverified-fetch] — carries **Benjamin Graham's** own FAJ articles and the 1976 "A Conversation with Benjamin Graham," plus **Seth Klarman's** rare Q&A "Opportunities for Patient Investors" (with Jason Zweig, FAJ 66:5, Sept/Oct 2010). Access via CFA membership or per-article purchase.
- **[JasonZweig.com](https://jasonzweig.com/a-rediscovered-masterpiece-by-benjamin-graham/)** — ongoing · free — Zweig (editor of the revised *Intelligent Investor*) posts rediscovered **Graham** texts and backstory on his Klarman FAJ conversation; Graham-focused supplement with reliable sourcing.

## Harvesting notes
- **Bulk PDF paths.** Graham & Doddsville: the official index blocks bots (403) but issue PDFs under `business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/imce-uploads/` download freely (verified 200s up to ~5 MB); the mirror's archive page covers 2006–2018 with direct links — build a URL list, ~45 issues to date. Heilbrunn's Graham lectures follow one filename pattern (`...Lecture1.pdf` → `Lecture10.pdf`), and the Graham-Newman letters sit on the same page — a one-shot scripted download.
- **Paywalls and dormant titles.** Value Investor Insight: subscription or per-issue "BUY ISSUE" purchases across the 2004–present archive; free samples require email registration. OID: dormant since May 2012, no online store — email the publisher via oid.com for back-issue offers. FAJ: CFA membership or Taylor & Francis per-article fees. Everything else in Tiers 1–3 is free.
- **Transcripts.** Value Investing with Legends publishes no official transcripts — auto-transcribe the Libsyn MP3s; for Masters in Business use Ritholtz's free full transcripts rather than scraping Bloomberg; Talks at Google and Ivey videos have YouTube captions, and MOI Global's free edited transcripts are cleaner for the two 2017 Google talks.
- **Dedup warning — syndication is rampant.** G&D interviews are re-hosted by ValueWalk, GuruFocus, MarketFolly, and by the investors themselves (Himalaya hosts the Li Lu issue; chaiwithpabrai.com hosts Pabrai's); the Talks at Google video and the MOI transcript are the same event; WealthTrack re-airs "encore" Greenblatt episodes. Key every item on (investor, event/issue, season-year), not on URL.
- **csinvesting.org is unstable** (spam homepage, intermittent 403s in 2026) — treat Wayback Machine snapshots as the canonical harvest source and record original post URLs for provenance.
- **SEC EDGAR as structured supplement.** Full-text search covers 2001–present with a JSON API (`efts.sec.gov`) — pull quarterly 13F-HR holdings and occasional 13D letters for Baupost (Klarman), Gotham (Greenblatt), Himalaya (Li Lu), and Dalal Street/Pabrai Funds; these are firm filings, not prose interviews, so pipe them into a separate "filings" stream. Baupost's partnership letters are not on EDGAR — don't expect letter text there (the 1996–2001 Baupost Fund N-30D letters are the exception; see the Klarman file).
- **FRASER as historical supplement.** Full-text search the *Commercial and Financial Chronicle* for contemporaneous prints of Graham's March 1955 Senate testimony and other 1930s–50s Graham speeches; the site's own search UI (not scripted queries) is the reliable way in.
- **Respect the rips rule.** Compiled PDFs of Lynch's Worth columns, OID single interviews, and full VII issues circulate on scribd/pdfcoffee — exclude them from automated harvest; use library databases, publisher back-issue purchase, or the excerpt/pointer sites (Novel Investor) instead.

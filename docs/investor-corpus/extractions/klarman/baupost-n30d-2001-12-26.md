## Concepts

- **Portfolio hedging through derivatives**: The Fund employed options (calls and puts) for both hedging and non-hedging purposes, and used forward foreign currency contracts and equity swap contracts to manage risk exposure across foreign and equity positions.
  > "The Fund may either write or purchase call and put options for both hedging and non-hedging activities." @chars 12080

- **Multi-instrument liquidity and credit management**: Baupost deployed repurchase agreements (with creditworthiness screening), securities lending (with fee income), and forward currency contracts as overlapping mechanisms to maintain liquidity and generate returns across asset classes.
  > "The Fund may enter into repurchase agreements with institutions that The Baupost Group L.L.C. ("Baupost") has determined are creditworthy." @chars 11447

- **Orderly, phased liquidation as risk management**: Rather than a single distribution, the Trustees structured the wind-down as a partial liquidating distribution followed by a final distribution roughly ten weeks later, giving the Fund time to sell down remaining positions in an orderly fashion.
  > "Net assets were distributed to shareholders in the form of a partial liquidating distribution paid August 10, 2001 to shareholders of record August 1, 2001 and a final liquidating distribution paid October 31, 2001 to shareholders of record that day." @chars 7601

- **Securities lending counterparty risk**: Even in a purely factual notes section, the Fund's own risk disclosure frames securities lending explicitly as extension-of-credit risk, consistent with Klarman's broader emphasis on always naming the downside of any yield-generating activity.
  > "As with other extensions of credit, the Fund bears the risk of delay in recovery or even loss of rights in the collateral should the borrower of the securities fail financially." @chars 13089-13260

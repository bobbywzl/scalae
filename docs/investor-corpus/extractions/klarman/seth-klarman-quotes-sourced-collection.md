## Concepts

- **title**: Margin of Safety — a protective cushion built into investment analysis that accounts for uncertainty and estimation error
  > "Margin of safety is simply the idea that you want room to be wrong." @line 153

- **title**: Risk Relativity — risk is not an inherent property of a security but emerges from the price paid relative to intrinsic value
  > "Risk is not inherent in an investment; it is always relative to the price paid." @line 545

- **title**: Capital Preservation — the foundational duty of investors before pursuing growth or returns
  > "The first and foremost responsibility of every investor is preservation of capital." @line 857

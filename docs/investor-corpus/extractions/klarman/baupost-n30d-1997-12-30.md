## Concepts

- **title**: Margin of safety — the core principle of value investing requiring investment in undervalued securities to provide protection against analysis errors and market downturns
  > "The main underlying principle of value investing is that you should invest in undervalued securities because they alone offer a margin of safety." @line 258

- **title**: The free option on recovery — purchasing assets at discounts to intrinsic value creates asymmetric payoff where loss is capped but gain is uncapped
  > "If you have limited downside risk from your purchase price, you have what is effectively a free option on the recovery of that business and/or the restoration of that stock to investor favor." @line 264

- **title**: Information scarcity as competitive advantage source — markets with poor disclosure standards and limited analyst coverage create outsized returns for rigorous analysts who can navigate opacity
  > "The biggest profits are always available (just as they have been in the U.S.) when competition and information are scarce." @line 295

- **title**: Value realization in European markets — thesis that major European markets are entering period of price discovery and normalization toward fair value
  > "We believe that we are at the beginning of a period of value realization in a number of these markets, and Baupost now has the capability to identify and rigorously analyze and monitor opportunities in foreign countries." @line 248

- **title**: Cash and hedges are both costly but complementary forms of downside insurance — the tradeoff of holding "never enough" of either during an actual downturn because both carry a running cost in normal times
  > "We hold both, although never enough in a downturn, because both are costly." @line 192

- **title**: Value investing works precisely because it tells you what to analyze (price vs. value) and what to do (buy at discount, sell near full value) when other disciplines lose their bearings — evidence: momentum investors can't find momentum, growth investors worry about slowdown, technical analysts dislike their charts in a downturn
  > "the value investing discipline tells you exactly what to analyze, price versus value, and then what to do, buy at a considerable discount and sell near full value." @line 228-230

## Concepts

- **title**: Margin of safety — A cushion created by purchasing assets at a discount that accommodates error, bad luck, and market volatility. Test: Does the purchase price substantially discount the calculated intrinsic value?
  > "Buying at a discount creates a margin of safety for the investor—room for imprecision, error, bad luck or the vicissitudes of volatile markets and economies." @line 150-152

- **title**: Value versus speculation — Value investors analyze securities as fractional ownership stakes in real businesses; speculators treat them as pieces of paper to trade. Question: Are you evaluating underlying business economics or chasing momentum?
  > "Unlike speculators, who think of securities as pieces of paper that you trade, value investors evaluate securities as fractional ownership of, or debt claims on, real businesses." @line 225-227

- **title**: Risk avoidance as primary objective — Traditional finance targets return; value investors first focus on risk and only then determine if projected returns justify taking each particular risk.
  > "The best investors do not target return; they focus first on risk, and only then decide whether the projected return justifies taking each particular risk." @line 35-37

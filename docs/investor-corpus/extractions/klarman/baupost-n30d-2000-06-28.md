## Concepts

- **title**: Margin of Safety — investing at substantial discount to calculated intrinsic value to protect against errors in analysis
  > "a value philosophy which attempts to create a margin of safety in each of our holdings" @line 143

- **title**: Value Philosophy Invariance — conviction that value investing discipline transcends market sentiment cycles and outperforms long-term
  > "A value philosophy never goes out of style. When sentiment towards undervalued sectors of the market is at its nadir, it is the best time to be buying value." @line 128

- **title**: Competitive Edge Requirement — investor must possess information, analysis, or seller-psychology advantage vs. counterparties in each position
  > "In effect, we seek an edge in all of our investments, a reason to believe we will have the wind at our backs, not in our faces, as we seek good investment returns over time with limited risk." @line 155

- **title**: Event-Driven Catalyst — specific trigger (restructuring, corporate action, market dislocation) required to realize gap between market price and intrinsic value

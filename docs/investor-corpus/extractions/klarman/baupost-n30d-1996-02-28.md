## Concepts

- **title**: Catalyst-driven value realization — mechanism for reducing portfolio volatility and eliminating dependence on broad market movements
  > "Positions with catalysts tend to lag a rapidly rising stock market (like this past year's) and outperform a lackluster or declining one (like we used to have every few years!)." @line 115

- **title**: Absolute returns philosophy versus relative performance — commitment to delivering positive returns independent of market regime
  > "The Baupost Fund is managed with the intention of earning good absolute returns regardless of how any particular financial market performs." @line 107

- **title**: Valuation paradigm shifts and dangerous consensus ideas — risk that conventional wisdom becomes self-perpetuating until reversal — mechanism: Buffett observation about theories
  > "Warren Buffett has pointed out that legitimate theories frequently lie at the root of financial excesses; good ideas are simply carried too far." @line 206

- **title**: Cyclicality of fear and greed as an agricultural metaphor — lean years sow the seeds of fat years and vice versa, a structural argument against extrapolating recent conditions indefinitely
  > "Just as seeds are sown during the seven lean years that allow the seven fat years to ensue, so does the reverse hold true." @line 196

- **title**: Willingness to underperform in bull markets as the price of protecting capital in bad ones — value discipline is defined by what it foregoes, not merely what it captures
  > "The true investment challenge is to perform well in difficult times. It is unfortunately not possible to reliably predict when those times might be. The cost of performing well in bad times can be relative underperformance in good times." @line 220

- **title**: Buy-too-soon risk as the central occupational hazard of value investing — discipline requires accepting that undervalued securities can become more undervalued before recovering
  > "Sometimes cheap stocks become a whole lot cheaper; it simply hasn't happened lately." @line 216

## Concepts

- **title**: Internet bubble as inflection point — Narrative-driven valuations becoming unsustainable as reality diverges from story
  > "Today, a popular tale involves more elements than growth alone; a good story stock must occupy a compelling and growing business niche, and possess strong market share, an able management, present or foreseeable earnings (or at least cash flow), and results that repeatedly exceed analyst expectations." @line 165

- **title**: Mr. Market's manic-depressive nature — Price volatility as opportunity source for disciplined value investors
  > "A manic fellow, Mr. Market will sometimes become exuberant, other times depressed. Investors who look to Mr. Market for advice will inevitably do the wrong thing at the wrong time. Investors who attempt to profit from Mr. Market's manic depressive nature will be successful over the long run." @line 214

- **title**: Margin of safety in senior debt — Creditor priority protection during corporate restructuring
  > "We also appreciate the margin of safety inherent in owning senior debt securities during a workout process, where equity receives the residual only after debtholder claims are satisfied." @line 269

- **title**: The "story stock" life cycle — Klarman describes a full narrative arc (compelling niche → market share → manipulated earnings → stock options wealth effect → eventual unraveling with margin calls and index deletion) as a repeatable pattern rather than a one-off phenomenon
  > "The unraveling of the virtuous circle of growth is not pretty, with earnings shortfalls, plunging share prices, employees with under-water options jumping ship, overzealous shareholders receiving margin calls, accounting chicanery exposed, lawsuits filed, and, to come full circle, the final insult of deletion from the relevant major market index." @line 179-183

- **title**: Extreme volatility as a double-edged risk — investors must prepare psychologically and financially (no margin debt) for mark-to-market losses even when the underlying long-term thesis is sound
  > "the price swings are so severe and swift, and not always in the desired direction, that investors must be braced for mark to market losses. Those sufficiently disciplined and unwavering will be generously rewarded." @line 233-235

- **title**: Market cycles and emotional counterintuition — The inevitability of wide price swings paired with the natural human instinct to sell in downturns and buy in upswings (inverse to profit)
  > "The stock market always has been, and always will be, subject to wide degrees of fluctuations." @chars 1315

- **title**: Selection vs. timing as two distinct disciplines — evidence: stock/bond selection alone is not the "backbone" of a program; the deeper discipline is deciding when to buy and when to sell

- **title**: Pre-arranged plan as a hedge against pro-cyclical panic selling — evidence: investors without a plan fail to buy at lows and instead add to downward pressure by selling near the bottom

## Question patterns

- **pattern**: Where are the cheapest stocks available across all markets and industries? — askWhen: during valuation phase, anchor: bargain-hunting

- **pattern**: Is this area's unpopularity permanent or temporary in nature? — askWhen: evaluating contrarian positions, anchor: risk-assessment
  > "We search for nations or industries where the stock prices are extremely low and they are only extremely low when there are good reasons for it." @line 135

- **pattern**: How will this company or sector perform under inflation and increased government intervention? — askWhen: risk-analysis phase, anchor: macro-conditions

- **directive**: Search global markets systematically for stocks priced lowest relative to estimated value using standard security analysis — queryShapes: price-to-value ratio, global coverage, sourcePriority: fundamentals over sentiment

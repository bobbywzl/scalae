## Other

- **Howard A. Newman's promotion and the Newman & Graham partnership**: The letter narrates a governance restructuring in unusual personal detail — Howard A. Newman moving from Vice President to Vice President & Treasurer, Jerome A. Newman becoming President, and Benjamin Graham stepping up to Chairman of the Board — explicitly noting Howard was "a General Partner of Newman & Graham," a related but separate partnership, showing how family and firm structures interlocked across Graham's business orbit.
  > "Mr. Howard A. Newman is presently Vice President and a Director of the Corporation, and a General Partner of Newman & Graham." @lines 67-68

- **Graham's move to Chairman of the Board**: This letter documents a specific inflection point in Graham's own career trajectory within the firm he co-founded — stepping back from the President title (held since at least 1946) into the Chairman role, with Jerome A. Newman taking over as President, even as Graham continued signing the letter as "President" at the time of writing (the change was pending board/shareholder approval at the meeting).
  > "Under the new arrangement Benjamin Graham will become Chairman of the Board, Jerome A. Newman will become President and Howard A. Newman will become Vice President and Treasurer of the Corporation." @lines 69-71

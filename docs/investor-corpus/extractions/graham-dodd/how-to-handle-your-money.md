## Concepts

- **title**: Dollar Averaging (Systematic Cost Averaging) — mechanism: investing fixed dollar amounts at regular intervals reduces average cost basis and removes emotion from timing
  > "By investing the same amount of money at regular intervals~say, every three months—you get two advantages. One is that over the years your investment reflects the average market price rather than the high market levels—which is where you are likely to buy if you follow the crowd." @line 415

- **title**: Fear-Driven Selling at Market Bottoms — mechanism: psychological factors override rational analysis, causing losses at worst times
  > "Most people sell stocks at low prices not because they have to but because they are scared." @line 243

- **title**: Investment Company Shares for Small Investors — mechanism: democratizes diversification for those with limited capital or expertise
  > "Perhaps I should correct my previous statement a little bit by saying that it is possible for the small man, the $5,000-a-vear man, who wishes to build up an interest in common stocks out of his rather modest savings, to do so reasonably well by following one of these accumulation programs in the investment-fund area—" @line 120

- **title**: Common Stocks as Inflation Hedge — mechanism: equity ownership provides protection against currency debasement and inflation
  > "Because common stocks have the advantage, in the first instance, of representing sound, growing investments in the American economy, with a higher return than you would get on bonds, and, secondly, because they do carry some measure of protection against inflation" @line 31

- **title**: Knowledge Reverses Apparent Risk — mechanism: purchasing unpopular securities at low prices, with skill, involves less risk than buying well-known stocks at high prices
  > "But when you consider the matter carefully you'll see that the purchase of securities that are not so well known at low prices—which is what a man wants to use his particular skill for—does not really involve more risk." @line 472

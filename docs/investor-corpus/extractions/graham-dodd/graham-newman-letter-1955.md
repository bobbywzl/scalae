## Concepts

- **title**: Realized vs. Unrealized Accounting Separation — fund distinguishes actual cash-realized profits from mark-to-market appreciation changes to assess true operating performance and limit management compensation to realized metrics only
  > "Net realized profits for the fiscal year (without reflecting the decrease in unrealized appreciation) amounted to $272.99 per share." @line 28-29

- **title**: Performance-Linked Management Compensation with Depreciation Clawback — officer bonus tied to realized income excess over threshold, but reduced dollar-for-dollar by unrealized depreciation, creating incentive to avoid valuation declines
  > "additional compensation is payable to them as a group of 20% of the excess of the realized net income in each year as determined at the close thereof (less the net unrealized depreciation, if any, in the value of the investments at the year end which had accrued subsequent to January 31, 1948)" @line 413-417

- **title**: Regulated Investment Company Tax Election and Mandatory Distribution Policy — fund must distribute all net income annually to shareholders to avoid corporate-level taxation, eliminating discretion over earnings retention
  > "The Corporation has elected to be taxed as a "regulated investment company" under the Internal Revenue Code, and as such will be liable to Federal taxes on income on such amount only as is represented by its undistributed net long term capital gains" @line 447-450

- **title**: Market Value Revaluation with Explicit Caveat on Realizability — securities marked to quoted prices at period end, but auditor notes these do not represent actual proceeds expected from sale

## Concepts

- **title**: Margin of Safety — test: enterprise value far exceeds security price; evidence: security offers protection from unforeseen losses through conservative valuation approach
  > "Its chief reliance is on a margin of safety that grows out of a small ratio of debt to total real value of the enterprise." @line 98-99

- **title**: Individual Risk vs. Aggregate Risk — test: diversified portfolio risk differs from single security; evidence: single stocks carry higher risk than single bonds, but diversified stocks historically carried less aggregate risk
  > "This point grows out of the basic difference between individual risk and overall or group risk." @line 154

- **title**: Pathology of Market Prices — test: understand reasons securities trade below value; evidence: causes include depressed earnings, low dividends, excess cash, litigation, capital structure issues
  > "They range from obvious causes, such as an unduly low dividend or a temporary setback in earnings, to more subtle and special conditions such as too much common stock in the capital structure or even too much cash in the bank." @line 217-220

- **title**: Prescientific vs. Scientific Investment Methods — test: methodology rigor and measurability; evidence: bond analysis achieves near-actuarial precision; growth analysis lacks dependable measurement standards
  > "In the growth stock field, the concept of margin of safety loses the clarity and the primacy it enjoys in those other two classes of security analysis." @line 279-281

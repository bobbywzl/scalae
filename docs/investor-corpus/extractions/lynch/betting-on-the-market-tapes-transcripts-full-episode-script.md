## Concepts

- **title**: "Understandable business over complex tech" — preference for simple, durable consumer products the investor can personally evaluate (a donut, a cup of coffee) over businesses whose value depends on technology he cannot judge
  > "If somebody invents a new logic chip, I'm not going to know about it. No one's every going to invent a better donut." @line 740

- **title**: "Conviction sized against consensus" — willingness to make an unpopular, "crazy"-looking position the fund's single largest holding when personal analysis diverged from prevailing sentiment, rather than sizing to match consensus comfort
  > "This was the largest position in my fund, in Magellan, in 1982, and everybody thought I was crazy." @line 780

- **title**: "Winners mature over years, not weeks" — the payoff on a stock pick typically showed up in the third or fourth year of ownership, not the third week or month, so short-horizon impatience is what forfeits the gain
  > "My best stocks have been the third year I bought them... not the third week, not the third month, the third, fourth year." @line 884

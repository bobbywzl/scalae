## Concepts
- **title**: Green shoe allotment — mechanism allowing underwriter to sell additional shares (typically 10% of original offering) when stock performs well post-IPO, named for defunct Green Shoe Corp.
  > "The underwriter, however, reserves the right to sell an additional amount of shares, usually about 10% of the offering shares, at the time of the offering." @line 38

- **title**: Leaving something on the table — underwriter strategy of deliberate underpricing to ensure initial buyers profit, building confidence in underwriter competence
  > "process is known as "leaving something on the table."" @line 36

- **title**: Use-of-proceeds test for insider conviction — whether IPO proceeds flow into the company (growth capital) versus into insiders' pockets (cashing out) signals management's own confidence in the business
  > "Whenever I see insiders using the IPO as an excuse to cash out, I ask myself: If they have no faith in the future of their company, why should I?" @line 124-126

## Concepts

- **title**: The "Easy to Follow" Company Filter — principle that sustainable competitive advantage and predictable cash flow are most reliably found in simple, comprehensible business models where an investor can construct a point of reference independent of market price
  > "You don't need a Ph.D. and a microscope to figure this story out, and there aren't 14 different divisions going off in14 different directions." @chars 1151

- **title**: Management Track Record in Acquisition Integration — tenure and demonstrated execution capability as indicator of ability to create value through inorganic growth
  > "They can take over a park that's down at the heels, renovate, spiff things up, advertise, and cut the overhead by folding the management into the Cedar Fair management." @chars 4849

- **title**: Off-Season Price Weakness With No News Flow — a structural feature of seasonal businesses whereby a stock can fall during the closed months purely on macro fear, with zero possibility that operating results have actually changed, since there are no operating results to report during that stretch
  > "so nothing is going to happen to affect the company's prospects for a prosperous1995 and beyond" @chars 2172

- **title**: Discipline of Sticking to a Small Number of Understood Companies — the counter-lesson to over-diversification: rather than spreading capital across exotic funds one doesn't understand, an investor should become deeply expert in four or five simple, followable businesses
  > "No investor can expect to be an expert in hundreds of stocks, but it's possible to become an expert in four or five that are easy to follow and monitor their progress and buy more shares when the opportunity arises." @chars 9840

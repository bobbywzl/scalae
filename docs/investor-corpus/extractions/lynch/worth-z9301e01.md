## Concepts

- **title**: Investor's Edge — personal knowledge or position advantage acquired through proximity to a business as customer, employee, or industry participant — thesis: the amateur's edge is "something you already have, not something you acquire"
  > "the investor's edge is something you already have, not something you acquire by listening to the latest tips from notable sources." @line 102

- **title**: Consumer's Edge — ability to identify business quality by observing which companies customers patronize and return to in restaurants, airports, shopping malls
  > "the consumer's edge, with which you can capitalize on your experiences in restaurants, airports, and shopping malls." @line 112

- **title**: Blundering Herd — large institutional investors whose collective buying/selling patterns are often mistaken and predictable, creating opportunity for contrarian individual investors
  > "A sizeable faction of this Thundering Herd could even be called the Blundering Herd." @line 29

- **title**: On-the-Job Edge — a working relationship with an industry or its suppliers/customers reveals business quality before it shows up in reported earnings
  > "Actually, there are two kinds of investor's edges: the on-the-job edge, in which you have a working relationship with an industry and the related companies with whom you do business" @line 109

- **title**: Celebrity Tip Dependency — following a well-known fund manager's disclosed holding without independent conviction defeats the purpose of the investor's edge and leaves the investor unable to judge when to hold or sell
  > "Since Gabelli doesn't give out his home phone number, you can't call him up to ask if he still likes Coca-Cola Enterprises and whether he views the price drop as an opportunity to buy more" @line 78-80

- **title**: Multibagger recognition via everyday products — spotting exponential winners (Microsoft's DOS bundled with every IBM PC) through direct product exposure rather than institutional research, though Lynch admits missing it himself
  > "I wish I'd noticed myself. The stock has been a 35-bagger." @line 180

- **name**: Price-to-Book Value (P/B) — formula: stock price divided by book value per share, threshold: below 1.0 signals deep discount in depressed markets

- **name**: Price-to-Earnings Ratio (P/E) — formula: stock price relative to annual earnings per share, reading: "very low price-to-earnings ratios" in recovery plays
  > "Many were selling at tantalizing prices: below book value and with very low price-to-earnings ratios" @chars 2740

- **name**: Delinquency Rate Trend (mortgages/loans) — formula: percentage of loans in default, bench: under 0.6% is ten-year low, six-year consecutive decline
  > "The delinquency rate on its mortgages has been going down for six years in a row, continuing to drop throughout the latest recession. Currently, the rate is less than six-tenths of one percent, a ten-year low" @chars 4800

- **name**: Sum-of-earnings-power valuation gap — formula: projected forward earnings x peer/market multiple, compared to current price, to size the undervaluation — reading: a stock trading well below its own market-multiple-implied price signals the "true value" isn't yet reflected

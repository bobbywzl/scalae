## Concepts

- **Title**: Win-Win Corporate Volunteerism — the paradox that corporate time investment in volunteering improves business outcomes (reduced absenteeism, office stress, job performance) while solving societal problems
  > "I've personally witnessed how these types of personal experiences can translate into reduced absenteeism and office stress, as well as improved job performance." @line 69-71

- **Title**: Volunteer Time as Underpriced Corporate Asset — employee time, unlike a cash donation, is a resource businesses systematically underinvest in mobilizing despite near-zero marginal cost and a documented productivity payoff
  > "Programs like these don't sap corporate resources; they enhance corporate resources." @line 103-104

- **Title**: Structural Time Squeeze on Volunteerism — rising employment and work hours (especially the growing share of women in the workforce) are structurally displacing the private time from which volunteer hours are drawn, independent of any decline in civic will
  > "Not only is a greater percentage of the population employed than ever before (with the biggest increase coming in the share of women in the workforce), but many people are also working longer and more stressful hours." @line 46-49

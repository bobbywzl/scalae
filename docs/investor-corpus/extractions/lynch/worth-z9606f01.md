## Concepts

- **title**: Capitalist Ecology — Industries dynamically replace decline; neither layoffs nor company failures are anomalies but the engine of renewal
  > "This process is hard on many, but ultimately, it is healthy." @line 24

- **title**: Productivity Dividend — Efficiency gains from restructuring can simultaneously fund employee raises, lower customer prices, and higher shareholder returns
  > "Let's say it builds a new plant, where the same work crew from the old plant can increase the output by 15 percent. It can use that 15 percent to give employees a 5 percent raise, making the workers happy; to lower prices by 5 percent, making customers happy; and to still increase profits, making the shareholders happy." @line 62

- **title**: Consumer Sovereignty Drives Restructuring — Workers themselves, as customers demanding low prices and quality, drive competitive pressure on employers; not CEO greed but consumer choice mandates efficiency
  > "All of us are looking for the best deals in clothing, computers, and telephone service and rewarding the high-quality, low-cost providers with our business." @line 44

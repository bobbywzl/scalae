- **title**: Sponsorship distribution as a durable moat — a distribution system where corporate employers actively promote and payroll-deduct a product for their workers is far stickier than ordinary retail sales and hard for challengers to replicate — test: What fraction of the addressable employer base (e.g. exchange-listed corporations) already participates in the sponsorship/payroll-deduction system?

- **title**: Product-line broadening to offset a maturing core product — as the original hit product's growth slows (cancer insurance in Japan and the U.S.), a durable operator launches adjacent products through the same trusted distribution channel rather than chasing new unrelated markets — test: Is new product revenue (noncancer policies, Super Care) growing as a share of total sales while using the existing distribution/sponsorship relationships?

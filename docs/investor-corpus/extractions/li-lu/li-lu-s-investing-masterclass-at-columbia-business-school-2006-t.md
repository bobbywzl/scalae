## Concepts

- **title**: Business Owner Mentality — psychological shift from perceiving stocks as trading instruments to seeing them as fractional ownership in operating enterprises
  > "All three come from the perception that you are a business owner, not a paper holder." @line 35

- **title**: Margin of Safety — quantitative and qualitative buffer protecting against forecast errors and unknown risks when one lacks operational control
  > "Since you only own a small part of the company and can't control it, you require a margin of safety as self-defense." @line 35

- **title**: Competitive Advantage — durable structural edge (switching costs, brand, network effects, government-protected access) that compounds over multi-decade holding periods
  > "Virtually all companies go through a similar transformation.. From a company that came from nowhere, that little by little began to gain an advantage until they hit a breaking point and usurped the established players becoming a monopoly." @line 174

- **title**: Lollapalooza Effect — Munger's concept of combining multiple reinforcing insights across disciplines to identify outlier investment opportunities
  > "It requires you to combine a lot of insights and factors, which Munger calls the Lollapalooza effect." @line 141

- **title**: Investigative Research as Core Skill — deep, forensic examination of company history, management biography, litigation, and stakeholder sentiment before capital commitment
  > "Being an investor is really being an investigative journalist. Going through all those questions, one has to find the answers quickly and never settle with one-sided answers." @line 72

- **title**: Circle of Competence — the bounded domain (geography, industry, time frame) within which one can forecast outcomes with confidence sufficiently high for conviction sizing
  > "Only by studying the company and industry for 15 years can I be sure to invest in them." @line 141

## Metrics

- **name**: Return on Deployed Capital (RODC) — operating earnings divided by the sum of working capital plus fixed assets; signals business productivity independent of capital structure
  > "With $850 million in sales and an operating margin of 13%, you get to roughly $110 million in operating earnings." @line 67

- **name**: Price-to-Book (P/B) Ratio — market capitalization relative to tangible net asset value; entry signal when below 1.0x for asset-heavy or cash-rich franchises
  > "The book value consists of tangible liquid assets, working capital and $100 million in real estate." @line 68

- **name**: Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio — valuation multiple; quality compounders at 5-15x P/E offer margin of safety if earnings growth persists
  > "It was never over 15 times P/E, but if you go from 5 P/E to 15 P/E, add 30% of growing earnings yearly and it adds up." @line 92

- **name**: Tangible Asset Coverage — liquidation value of cash, receivables, inventory, and real estate as percentage of market capitalization; downside protection floor
  > "The company has $300 million in book value, $275 million in working capital and with the results of previous years, we can probably add around $100 million more." @line 66

## Concepts

- **title**: Circle of Competence Boundaries — understanding: the competence circle's defining feature is its boundary, not its center; failure to know the limits is equivalent to not understanding
  > "The most important concept in the circle of competence is its boundary" @line 48

- **title**: Cognitive Bias as Systematic Barrier to Rational Investing — evidence: human cognition evolved for survival/reproduction, not market analysis; innate biases make objectivity exceptionally difficult without deliberate training
  > "our minds are the product of natural selection, and their main function is for us to survive and procreate" @line 47

- **title**: Intellectual Honesty as Prerequisite for Real Understanding — test: ability to articulate the strongest counterargument to one's own thesis better than the best informed opponent
  > "if you want to hold an opinion, then you must be able to refute it better than the smartest person you can find who disagrees with you" @line 48

- **title**: Knowledge Compounding Through Passion-Guided Learning — principle: passionate pursuit of a subject creates faster, more effective knowledge accumulation than disinterested study
  > "When you become really interested in something, you can accumulate knowledge about it faster, more effectively and better than anyone else" @line 52

- **title**: Margin of Safety as Protection Against Forecasting Error — function: a sufficient margin of safety allows profitable investment even when understanding is incomplete, because the price protection compensates for predictive uncertainty
  > "When the future isn't clear, you must choose those opportunities which are especially cheap" @line 55

- **title**: Industry Selection as Gating Function for Value Investing — principle: most companies operate in industries with unpredictable competitive outcomes; focus only on industries whose future structure can be forecasted
  > "most companies are too hard and predictions about them cannot be made" @line 58

- **title**: Market Economy as Potential Releaser — insight: market systems enable unconventional people to succeed in niches suited to their unique traits; no uniform template for successful entrepreneurs exists
  > "market economies have a very special property: they can release a person's true potential" @line 62
